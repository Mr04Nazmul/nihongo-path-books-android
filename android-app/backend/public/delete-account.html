Delete your Nihongo Path Books account

This deletes Nihongo application data including cloud bookmarks and progress. The shared Supabase sign-in identity used by the publisher’s other applications is preserved. Contact support for a separate shared-identity deletion request.

Open the Android app → Account → sign in → Delete account & study access → confirm Delete. This permanently removes your profile, sessions, credit balances, unlocks and account-linked reward/redemption history.
