Privacy policy / গোপনীয়তার নীতি

Effective: 6 November 2026. Publisher: Hossain Najmul (DevNazu).

Account and study data

We store your email address, display name, hashed session tokens, level credit balances, verified advertising transactions, redemption history, access expiry timestamps and basic access/security events in the private Supabase PostgreSQL books schema. Supabase Auth stores password hashes, never a readable password list. New accounts created through this app sign in immediately without a confirmation email or code. Forgotten passwords are handled through support after an ownership check. Shared project-wide email confirmation settings are unchanged. Android session credentials are encrypted using Android Keystore. Bookmarks and reading progress are stored on your device and can synchronize with your account, along with access and credits.

Advertising

The owner release uses Google AdMob rewarded advertisements; development builds may use sample advertisements. Advertisements are voluntary. When advertising is requested, Google may receive network information such as your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad/device identifiers, ad interactions, and SDK diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google describes its processing in its privacy policy. The advertising SDK starts after an explicit ad request and the applicable consent checks. Advertising rewards are recorded after successful reward verification. Reward balances and access expiry times are used to provide the selected time-limited book access. Unfinished, unavailable or failed ads do not deduct credits. You can open advertising privacy choices in the app's About screen.

Study content and security

The app reads educational material from devnazu.online through the study service after checking level access. The existing public website remains public. Supported Android screenshots and screen recording are blocked with FLAG_SECURE; this does not prevent external cameras or all modified-device attacks.

Shared authentication account

Supabase email/password authentication is shared with the publisher’s other applications. Deleting Nihongo Path Books data removes this app’s profile, bookmarks, reading progress, sessions and access, but preserves the shared sign-in identity. Password reset affects that shared identity. Contact support for deletion requests covering the shared identity.

Invitation bonus

When an eligible new user joins using a valid referral, both the inviter and the invited user receive seven days of access to all available books. This is a referral access reward, separate from advertising rewards. We process referral codes, account identifiers, eligibility checks, referral records and access expiry times to grant rewards and prevent self-referrals, duplicate claims and abuse. We retain a one-way identity digest and bonus record after app-data deletion to prevent repeated claims; this record has no email or display name. Your existing shared referral code may be reused without changing other applications’ points.

Retention and deletion

Account records remain until you delete the account. Sessions expire after 30 days. You may delete your account in Account → Delete account in the Android app. This removes profile, sessions, credits, active access and account-linked histories. Local bookmarks can be cleared through Android app data settings. For assistance, contact the publisher using the support page or email najmulhossain20244@gmail.com. Developer website: https://devnazu.jp.
