Privacy policy / গোপনীয়তার নীতি

Effective: 29 October 2026. Publisher: Hossain Najmul (DevNazu).

Account and study data

We store your email address, display name, salted scrypt password hash, hashed session tokens, level credit balances, verified advertising transactions, redemption history, access expiry timestamps and basic access/security events in Neon PostgreSQL. Android session credentials are encrypted using Android Keystore. Bookmarks and reading progress are stored on your device; access and credits synchronize through your account.

Advertising

The Android development build uses Google AdMob sample advertisements. Advertisements are voluntary. Google may process device and advertising information according to its privacy terms and your consent choices. Only a verified Google server callback can issue a rewarded level credit. Unfinished, unavailable or failed ads do not deduct credits. You can open advertising privacy choices in the app's Account screen.

Study content and security

The app reads educational material from devnazu.online through the study service after checking level access. The existing public website remains public. Supported Android screenshots and screen recording are blocked with FLAG_SECURE; this does not prevent external cameras or all modified-device attacks.

Shared authentication account

Supabase email/password authentication is shared with the publisher’s other applications. Deleting Nihongo Path Books data removes this app’s profile, bookmarks, reading progress, sessions and access, but preserves the shared sign-in identity. Password reset affects that shared identity. Contact support for deletion requests covering the shared identity.

Invitation bonus

A new confirmed account using a valid invitation can receive five N5 credits once. These are invitation credits, not verified advertisement rewards. We retain a one-way identity digest and bonus record after app-data deletion to prevent repeated claims; this record has no email or display name. Your existing shared referral code may be reused without changing other applications’ points.

Retention and deletion

Account records remain until you delete the account. Sessions expire after 30 days. You may delete your account in Account → Delete account in the Android app. This removes profile, sessions, credits, active access and account-linked histories. Local bookmarks can be cleared through Android app data settings. For assistance, contact the publisher using the support page.
